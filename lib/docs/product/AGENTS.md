## Library product docs conventions

- Product docs are at the System level. They state the problem the library
  solves, what it offers every product, its people and their agents, and
  what it leaves out.
- How the library is built belongs in [architecture](../architecture/), and
  behavior a product or an agent observes belongs in
  [contracts](../contracts/). A product doc names those topics in a sentence
  and links to their doc.
- A feature the library as a whole leaves out is listed under Scope in
  [library.md](library.md).
